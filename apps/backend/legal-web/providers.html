Legal & privacy

Your data, service bookings and rights

Updated 28 October 2026 · 2026-10-28-pilot-draft-7

These documents describe the OneCall pilot and its optional Paystack checkout capability. The operator's registered legal name, business address and final retention schedule must be confirmed before public launch. Payment availability is shown in the app; a test payment does not collect real money. Protected payment holds, advertising rewards and AI assistance are not enabled.

Read the terms before using OneCall. The privacy notice explains how information is handled; reading it is not consent to optional marketing, advertising or location sharing. Those uses require their own explanation and permission where applicable.

Provider rules

Applications, coverage, quotations, conduct and customer information.

Application and approval

Providers use the Provider Workspace within the same OneCall app. Submit accurate display-name, service, coverage and applicable vehicle information. An application is pending until operations approves it. Only accept jobs you can lawfully and competently fulfil in your approved coverage. Current approval does not represent a completed NIN, bank, police or professional-certification check.

Performing work

Give a clear written quote and confirm the scope, parts, materials and timing. Start only after the customer accepts the current quote. Obtain agreement for scope changes. Keep status accurate, communicate delays and handle customer concerns respectfully. A completed status does not authorise a payout or override a complaint. Applicable fees or commission must be agreed and disclosed before being charged; none is created merely by this draft.

Customer privacy

Use customer addresses, contact details and messages only for the assigned job and its legitimate follow-up. Do not sell them, share them for unrelated purposes or use them for unsolicited marketing. Keep any justified offline records secure and no longer than needed. Your legal responsibilities for independently retained information depend on how you use it. Report a suspected data leak to OneCall promptly. Optional location sharing is limited to the assigned in-progress job.

Parcel shop operators

Parcel shop approval is separate from ordinary service-provider approval. Operations must check identity, physical shop and custody arrangements and record a review reference. Approval is not an automated NIN verification or a guarantee against loss. The app does not collect NIN documents in this flow.

Origin shops propose route prices, capacity and transit estimates; an independent administrator must confirm both shops, pricing, custody arrangements and permitted-item restrictions before activating each route. Record only physical handovers you have verified. Do not release a parcel without the proper collection code. Keep recipient information and codes private, use them only for the shipment, and report custody incidents through Support. Suspended shops cannot receive new bookings or perform custody updates until Operations resolves the issue.

Contact & data requests

You can request account deletion, access, correction or export without signing in or reinstalling the app. Requests require identity verification and manual handling; this page does not automatically delete data.

Prepare email request

Send to onecalltech@gmail.com. If an email app does not open, copy this address into your email service. Never send passwords, OTPs, NIN or bank details.

Reference authorities: Nigeria Data Protection Commission · FCCPC consumer rights.
