Legal & privacy

Your data, service bookings and rights

Updated 13 October 2026 · 2026-10-13-pilot-draft-3

These documents describe the current OneCall pilot. The operator's registered legal name, business address and final retention schedule must be confirmed before public launch. Online payments, payment protection, advertising rewards and AI assistance are not enabled in this pilot.

Read the terms before using OneCall. The privacy notice explains how information is handled; reading it is not consent to optional marketing, advertising or location sharing. Those uses require their own explanation and permission where applicable.

Cookies & device storage

Session credentials, preferences and temporary data on your device.

Essential storage

The mobile app uses secure-storage facilities for the saved session and basic user profile. Device/browser preferences remember the selected state and city. Forms may hold unsent text temporarily while open; this draft does not promise saved booking drafts after restarting the app. Signing out removes the saved OneCall session. Clearing app or browser data may also remove preferences and require a new sign-in; it does not delete your server-side account.

Advertising and analytics

The current pilot does not enable AdMob advertising or a third-party behavioural analytics SDK. Third-party map or hosting services may still process network or device information necessary to serve their features. Before adding non-essential trackers, OneCall must update its disclosures and implement applicable permission and consent controls. Declining an optional rewarded ad will not block ordinary booking or account access.

Contact & data requests

You can request account deletion, access, correction or export without signing in or reinstalling the app. Requests require identity verification and manual handling; this page does not automatically delete data.

Prepare email request

Send to onecalltech@gmail.com. If an email app does not open, copy this address into your email service. Never send passwords, OTPs, NIN or bank details.

Reference authorities: Nigeria Data Protection Commission · FCCPC consumer rights.
