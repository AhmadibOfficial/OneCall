# OneCall — Legal & privacy

**Pilot draft for review**

Updated: 13 October 2026

Version: 2026-10-13-pilot-draft-3

These documents describe the current OneCall pilot. The operator's registered legal name, business address and final retention schedule must be confirmed before public launch. Online payments, payment protection, advertising rewards and AI assistance are not enabled in this pilot.

Read the terms before using OneCall. The privacy notice explains how information is handled; reading it is not consent to optional marketing, advertising or location sharing. Those uses require their own explanation and permission where applicable.

## Privacy policy

Information we use, who can see it and your privacy rights.

### 1. Who is responsible

OneCall connects customers with independent service providers in Nigeria. OneCall is responsible for the personal data used to operate its account, booking, provider workspace, support and administration services. The registered operator's legal identity and business address will be added to the final notice. Privacy contact: onecalltech@gmail.com.

The notice is prepared with reference to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable Nigeria Data Protection Commission directives, including the General Application and Implementation Directive 2025. Consumer rights also apply under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018.


### 2. Information used in the current pilot

- Account information: name, email, phone number, OneCall ID, account status and verification records. Passwords are stored as hashes; session credentials are used to keep accounts secure.
- Booking information: service, state and city, address, description, schedule, applicable pickup and destination details, provider assignment, quotes, quote acceptance and job status history.
- Account features: saved addresses, favourites, in-app notifications, booking messages and support requests and responses. Profile images and file metadata are processed if you use a supported image-upload feature.
- Provider information: provider or business display name, services, requested and approved coverage, approval status and vehicle description where applicable.
- Security and operational information: request times, verification attempts, IP address and user-agent information in verification audit records, and administrator operation records used to investigate misuse.
- Optional precise location: an assigned provider can choose to share location for an in-progress booking. The app requests device permission before collecting GPS coordinates. Entering an address manually does not require GPS permission.
- Optional Google or Apple sign-in, when configured: provider identifier, account identifier and provider-verified email. OneCall receives credentials to verify sign-in but does not store the provider ID token or your Google/Apple password. You can continue using email and password. Linking requires signing in to your existing OneCall account first.
- Sign-in attempt records: short-lived nonce, hashed attempt secret, expiry and completion times, used to prevent replay. Expired attempt records are removed when new attempts are created. Linked provider identifiers are retained with the account and included in applicable privacy request handling.
- Optional booking photos: up to six JPEG or PNG images per booking, visible only to the customer, assigned provider and authorised operators where needed for support. Images can contain original metadata. Avoid uploading identity documents or unrelated people; remove sensitive metadata before uploading. Photos are stored privately and are not public gallery links.
- Apple sign-in, when configured: encrypted refresh credentials are retained to check continued authorisation. Signed provider notices can remove a withdrawn link and revoke OneCall sessions. Browser sign-in results are encrypted and expire after five minutes; credentials never appear in callback URLs.
- Appearance preferences: text size and reduced motion are saved locally on the device. Device accessibility text scaling remains respected.
- Privacy requests: requests and support responses are recorded in your account. A password-protected basic account snapshot is available; it does not replace a full access request. A support response does not mean deletion has been completed. Deletion requires identity checks and review of lawful retention requirements.

### 3. Purposes and lawful bases

Information needed to create an account, arrange a booking, communicate about the job and provide support is used to deliver the requested service and perform the relevant contract. Security, abuse prevention and necessary operational improvements are assessed against our legitimate interests and your rights. Records may also be processed where an applicable legal obligation requires it.

Optional location sharing and any future optional marketing are explained separately, with consent where required. You can stop location sharing and change device permissions. A device permission is not permission for unrelated tracking or marketing.


### 4. Who can see information

- Customers and their assigned providers can access information needed for their booking. The available-jobs queue for unassigned providers excludes the customer's street address, description and pickup/destination details; those details are made available after assignment to perform the job.
- Authorised OneCall operations and support staff can access relevant records to review applications, manage bookings, investigate complaints and protect accounts. Such access is limited by permissions.
- Hosting, database, email and SMS vendors process the information needed to provide the services configured for the deployment. When maps are enabled or you choose to open an external map, the relevant map provider may process device/network information and coordinates under its own notice.
- Information may be disclosed to authorities or professional advisers when lawfully required or necessary to investigate a complaint or protect users. We do not sell personal data.

### 5. Location sharing

Provider GPS sharing is optional and is shown to the customer assigned to that in-progress booking. Sharing stops when the provider turns it off, leaves the tracking screen or backgrounds the app. The most recently submitted coordinate can remain stored after sharing stops; it is removed from the live booking-location record when the job is completed or cancelled. Historical backups may retain an earlier copy until they expire. The pilot does not provide continuous background location tracking.


### 6. Payments, identity checks, ads and AI

Online checkout, wallet funding, escrow, automatic refunds and provider bank payouts are not offered in this pilot. No payment-card details are requested through the pilot booking flow. Payments are planned for a later release.

The pilot provider application does not collect NIN, identity selfies, bank payout details or identity-verification certificates. Email/phone verification and provider approval do not mean a government identity or background check has been completed.

AdMob rewards and Amboy AI assistance are planned, not active. Before either is enabled, OneCall will explain the additional data recipients, purposes, permissions, retention and applicable choices. OneCall does not claim that future advertising or AI vendors are already covered by this pilot notice.


### 7. Hosting, international transfers and security

Hosting location depends on the selected deployment and its service providers. This draft does not claim that data is stored only or primarily in Nigeria. Before public launch, OneCall must identify processing locations and establish applicable safeguards for transfers outside Nigeria.

The software uses password hashing, account permissions, secure session storage and checks on booking ownership. Public deployments must use HTTPS and protected database and vendor credentials. Access to backups must be restricted. Please protect your device and report suspected account misuse; do not send passwords or verification codes to support.


### 8. Retention and deletion

Records are kept for the service purpose and any justified legal, security or complaint-handling need. The final category-by-category retention and backup-expiry schedule is still to be approved; this draft does not promise an automatic expiry that the software does not enforce.

You can remove a saved address and request correction, export or deletion of account information. Removing an address or signing out does not remove booking history or delete an account. On a verified account-deletion request, OneCall must identify data to delete or anonymise, explain any lawful retention and address copies held by service providers. Account deactivation alone is not data deletion.


### 9. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable law and its exceptions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability of personal data, and withdraw consent for consent-based uses. You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. These terms do not remove those rights.

Use Legal & privacy > Account deletion & data requests, in-app Support, or email onecalltech@gmail.com. Email is available even if you cannot sign in or have uninstalled the app. Specify the request and your registered contact or OneCall ID. We will verify identity proportionately before disclosing or deleting data. Do not send NIN, bank details, passwords or OTPs. Requests require human handling within applicable legal timelines; submitting a request does not instantly delete data.


### 10. Adults, automated decisions and changes

OneCall's intended marketplace users are adults aged 18 or above. Report suspected use by a child to support so it can be investigated with appropriate privacy safeguards.

Current matching uses service coverage and booking rules; provider approval is an operations decision. You can ask support to review an account restriction or a significant matching or approval issue. The pilot has no autonomous AI booking or provider-approval feature.

The version and revision date identify this draft. Material changes must be communicated appropriately, with fresh consent where a new optional use requires it. A notice update does not create blanket consent to a new purpose.


## Terms of service

Accounts, service requests, quotes, cancellations and fair use.

### 1. The marketplace

OneCall helps customers arrange services with independent providers. Providers perform the underlying work and are responsible for their competence, tools, applicable licences, conduct and agreed scope. OneCall remains responsible for its own platform conduct and obligations under applicable law. Availability depends on approved providers covering your area; a request is not a guarantee of attendance or a completed job.


### 2. Eligibility and accounts

You must be at least 18 and legally able to enter the relevant agreement. Give accurate information and keep credentials private. Do not share passwords or verification codes. Report unauthorised access promptly. Verification of email and phone establishes control of those contact methods; it is not a government identity check.


### 3. Requests, assignment and quotes

Describe the work, location, timing, access requirements and known safety issues accurately. A provider may be assigned through operations or accept an eligible request. Review the assigned provider and written quote before accepting. Confirm whether materials, parts, transport or other charges are included. A provider must obtain your agreement before additional work or charges; a quoted price is not permission for unspecified charges.

The app requires customer acceptance of the current provider quote before the provider can start work. Providers update job status, including completion; a provider's completed status is not proof that the customer has approved the quality of work and does not release money in this pilot.


### 4. Payments and cancellations

Online payments and payment protection are unavailable in this pilot. Agree any payment arrangement transparently with the provider and retain receipts. OneCall does not receive or hold those funds, process automatic refunds or guarantee recovery of a direct payment.

Use the cancellation action when available. If work has started or the action is unavailable, contact support promptly. Cancellation rights and any lawful, reasonable charges depend on the circumstances and applicable consumer law. The pilot does not automatically impose a cancellation fee. Do not treat a status change as an automatic refund or a waiver of consumer rights.


### 5. Conduct, content and safety

- Use the platform lawfully. Do not post fake jobs, harass others, discriminate, impersonate another user, upload malicious material or misuse private contact information.
- Keep job discussions and changes in booking messages where possible. Provide safe access and report hazards. OneCall is not an emergency-response service.
- Do not access another person's account or interfere with security or infrastructure. Security testing requires prior written authorisation.
- You retain rights to content you provide. Give OneCall only the permission needed to store, display and handle that content for the booking, support and security purposes described in the privacy notice. This permission does not authorise unrelated advertising or AI training.

### 6. Complaints, restrictions and liability

Contact support with the booking ID and relevant facts if a provider fails to attend, work is unsafe or the service differs from the agreement. Keep messages and receipts. OneCall may investigate and restrict an account for substantiated misuse, security risk or legal requirements; you may request review.

Nothing in these terms excludes rights, remedies or liability that Nigerian law does not permit to be excluded. This draft does not impose a blanket indemnity or an arbitrary liability cap. Access to FCCPC, NDPC, competent courts and other lawful remedies is preserved.


### 7. Governing law and updates

The applicable laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria govern the relevant platform relationship. You can seek support first without losing a right to approach a regulator or court. Mediation is voluntary where agreed. Material term changes must be made available before they apply, with appropriate notice and any required acceptance; publication alone does not prove an individual accepted a version.


## Payment availability

Online payments and payment protection are not active.

### Current pilot

OneCall's online checkout, wallet funding, provider payouts and protected payment holds are not offered in this pilot. Booking and quote acceptance do not debit an account. Direct payment arrangements are between the customer and provider; OneCall cannot issue an automatic refund for money it did not collect. Contact support with evidence if a service or payment arrangement causes a problem. Your statutory rights remain available.


### Before a future payment launch

OneCall must publish specific payment terms after confirming its processor and the implemented collection, fees, settlement, refunds and dispute workflow. Paystack is the proposed integration, but holding funds until completion, escrow, delayed settlement and automatic refunds require a supported and verified arrangement; a normal checkout alone does not establish those protections. Future terms will show fees and timing before payment and will not remove lawful bank, chargeback or consumer remedies.


## Consumer protection

Clear prices, fair treatment, quality and complaint channels.

### Fair service information

OneCall aims to give understandable service information and a clear quote before you agree to work. Provider approval does not guarantee every skill, licence or outcome. Ask for evidence relevant to the job and confirm the scope and price.


### Complaints and redress

Customers have applicable rights concerning fair information, reasonable cancellation and service quality under the FCCPA. Those rights are preserved even where the software lacks an automated remedy. Contact OneCall Support or onecalltech@gmail.com with the booking ID, agreed scope and relevant evidence. You may also contact the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission or seek other lawful remedies; using support is not a condition for exercising statutory rights.


## Cookies & device storage

Session credentials, preferences and temporary data on your device.

### Essential storage

The mobile app uses secure-storage facilities for the saved session and basic user profile. Device/browser preferences remember the selected state and city. Forms may hold unsent text temporarily while open; this draft does not promise saved booking drafts after restarting the app. Signing out removes the saved OneCall session. Clearing app or browser data may also remove preferences and require a new sign-in; it does not delete your server-side account.


### Advertising and analytics

The current pilot does not enable AdMob advertising or a third-party behavioural analytics SDK. Third-party map or hosting services may still process network or device information necessary to serve their features. Before adding non-essential trackers, OneCall must update its disclosures and implement applicable permission and consent controls. Declining an optional rewarded ad will not block ordinary booking or account access.


## Provider rules

Applications, coverage, quotations, conduct and customer information.

### Application and approval

Providers use the Provider Workspace within the same OneCall app. Submit accurate display-name, service, coverage and applicable vehicle information. An application is pending until operations approves it. Only accept jobs you can lawfully and competently fulfil in your approved coverage. Current approval does not represent a completed NIN, bank, police or professional-certification check.


### Performing work

Give a clear written quote and confirm the scope, parts, materials and timing. Start only after the customer accepts the current quote. Obtain agreement for scope changes. Keep status accurate, communicate delays and handle customer concerns respectfully. A completed status does not authorise a payout or override a complaint. Applicable fees or commission must be agreed and disclosed before being charged; none is created merely by this draft.


### Customer privacy

Use customer addresses, contact details and messages only for the assigned job and its legitimate follow-up. Do not sell them, share them for unrelated purposes or use them for unsolicited marketing. Keep any justified offline records secure and no longer than needed. Your legal responsibilities for independently retained information depend on how you use it. Report a suspected data leak to OneCall promptly. Optional location sharing is limited to the assigned in-progress job.


## Account deletion & data requests

Request deletion, access, correction or export without needing to sign in.

### How to request

Email onecalltech@gmail.com with the subject 'OneCall account deletion' or the type of privacy request. Include your registered email or phone number or OneCall ID, and explain what you want deleted, corrected or provided. You may email even if the app is uninstalled or the account is inaccessible. Signed-in users can also submit a privacy request through Support. Never include passwords, OTPs, NIN or bank details.


### What happens next

OneCall must verify the requester's identity, confirm the scope, and explain the applicable processing time and any justified retention. Deletion covers the account and associated data subject to lawful exceptions, not just a change to account status. This request route requires manual processing; it is not an automatic deletion tool and no data is deleted when you open the page or prepare an email.

Open bookings and complaints may need coordination, but they do not remove your ability to submit a request. Any retention of specific booking, security or complaint records must have a lawful reason and an explained period. Backup copies must expire under an approved schedule. Before public launch, the deletion/export procedure and response ownership must be tested end to end.
